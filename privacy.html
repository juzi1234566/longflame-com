Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version: you can read every page of this site without giving us anything. If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address and use it only to send you that newsletter.

Data controller

This website, longflame.com, is run by LongFlame (“we”, “us”), a brand of 五星东方（昆山）文化科技有限公司, a company registered in Kunshan, Jiangsu, China. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

Contact: hello@longflame.com

What we collect

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. That is the only thing we ask for.

We do not set cookies on this site, and it has no accounts.

Why we use it, and on what basis

We use your email address only to send you the LongFlame newsletter. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Our legal basis is your consent. We ask for it twice: after you enter your address, we send a confirmation email, and you are only subscribed once you click the link in it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing.

Who handles it for us

Our newsletter is run by Kit, an email service provider. Kit stores the subscriber list and sends the emails on our behalf, and it may process your data outside the UK and the European Economic Area. Until the sign-up form is switched on, an address typed into it is not sent anywhere; the form says so when you press the button.

Hosting and visit counts

These pages are static files served by a hosting provider. As with any website, the provider’s servers receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. We do not read those records.

This site does not currently load any analytics script. If we start counting visits, we will choose a tool that does not use cookies and update this page first.

Fonts

The typefaces on this site (EB Garamond and Inter, both under the SIL Open Font License) are served from longflame.com itself. Your browser does not contact any font service when you read these pages.

Links to other sites

Sources, photograph credits and our social media pages are external websites. Their own privacy policies apply once you follow a link.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address for as long as you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop sending you emails. If you would also like your address deleted entirely, write to us and we will do it.

Unsubscribing

Every email we send has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also write to hello@longflame.com.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, you have the right to ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To do any of these, write to hello@longflame.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO); in the EU, the authority in the country where you live.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
