About

LongFlame

LongFlame documents representative inheritors of China’s intangible cultural heritage. Every inheritor is visited and filmed in person, and each story is published only after the inheritor has reviewed it.

Each episode of the series visits one inheritor. The host appears only at the opening and the close; the rest of the time belongs to the inheritor.

This site keeps the text version of each episode and a record for each inheritor.
