LongFlame

Known in China as 太守寻非遗.

LongFlame documents representative inheritors of China’s intangible cultural heritage. Every inheritor is visited and filmed in person, and each story is published only after the inheritor has reviewed it.

Principles

  1. 01Filmed in person.Inheritors and their works are photographed and filmed on site.
  2. 02Reviewed by the inheritor.Every story is published only after the inheritor has approved it.
  3. 03Clearly labelled.Any AI-generated footage is marked as such.

For inheritors and partners

Representative inheritors and cultural institutions may contact us at hello@longflame.com.
