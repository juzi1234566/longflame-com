About · LongFlame

We visit the keepers of China’s intangible cultural heritage. Every story is published only after they have reviewed it.

The series

Original title 太守行纪

Each episode travels to one place and visits one representative inheritor of China’s intangible cultural heritage. The English title of the series has not been decided yet.

The host is a visitor and appears only at the opening and the close. The time in between belongs to the inheritor.

Episode 01 visits Xiangshui rice cultivation technique in Ning’an, Heilongjiang. It is in preparation and has not been filmed yet.

How we work

LongFlame · The inheritor

LongFlame
  • Visits, films and edits on site
  • Prepares the text version of each episode and the record of each inheritor
  • States as history only what reliable sources support, and says so when something is legend
  • Labels any AI-generated footage on screen
The inheritor
  • Is the host of the episode; we are the guests
  • Reviews their name, words and portrait before anything is published
  • Is shown with their own work, photographed as it is
  • Can ask for a correction at any time
