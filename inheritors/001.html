About the craft

Xiangshui rice cultivation technique
Element
Xiangshui rice cultivation technique
List
Heilongjiang Provincial List of Intangible Cultural Heritage, second batch (2009), traditional craftsmanship

  1. In June 2009 the People’s Government of Heilongjiang Province published the second batch of the provincial list of intangible cultural heritage. “Xiangshui rice cultivation technique” was listed under traditional craftsmanship, nominated by Mudanjiang City. [1]

  2. In 2007 China’s General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection and Quarantine approved Xiangshui rice as a protected geographical indication product (Announcement No. 15 of 2007). The protected area covers five townships of Ning’an: Bohai, Dongjingcheng, Jiangnan, Wolong and Lan’gang. [2][3]

  3. Growing conditions recorded in the local standard: at least 2,400 hours of sunshine a year, accumulated active temperature of at least 2,300 °C, a mean annual temperature of 3.8 °C and 550–600 mm of rain a year. Irrigation water comes from Jingpo Lake, on the upper Mudan River, and other clean, unpolluted sources. [3]

  4. In 2015 the Ministry of Agriculture designated the “Ning’an Xiangshui Rice Culture System, Heilongjiang” as one of the third batch of China Nationally Important Agricultural Heritage Systems. This is a list of agricultural heritage, not a list of intangible cultural heritage. [4]

  5. The account of Bohai in the New Book of Tang lists “the rice of Lucheng” among the things valued in the Bohai kingdom. Scholars disagree about where Lucheng was, and the claim that the rice of Lucheng is Xiangshui rice remains unsettled. [5][6]

  6. Local tradition holds that Xiangshui rice was sent to the court as tribute rice in past dynasties. No primary historical source for this has been found; it is a legend.

The rice year

Based on the technical requirements for Xiangshui rice as a geographical indication product (source 2).

  1. Photograph · Raising seedlings (3:2)SpringSeedlings are raised in pot trays, in dry beds under large and medium plastic tunnels.
  2. Photograph · Transplanting (3:2)Mid to late MayTransplanting begins once the daily mean temperature stays above 13 °C, usually between 15 and 25 May.
  3. Photograph · Irrigation (3:2)SummerShallow irrigation.
  4. Photograph · Harvest and drying (3:2)AutumnThe rice is harvested when ninety per cent of the grain is ripe, then dried.
  5. Photograph · Processing (3:2)ProcessingEach variety is harvested, threshed and milled separately.
